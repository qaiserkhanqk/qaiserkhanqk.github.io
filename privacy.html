Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy covers Qaiser Shorts Poster ("the app"), a private tool run by Qaiser Khan that uploads his own videos to his own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

What the app accesses

When the account owner signs in with Google or TikTok, the app receives permission to upload videos and to read basic channel or profile details (such as the channel name). It does not read, collect or store data about any other person.

How data is used

The app uses this access only to publish videos and captions that the account owner has prepared, and to confirm which account it is posting to. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer, in the macOS Keychain. Videos and captions stay on that computer until they are uploaded to the platform the owner chose.

Google user data

Qaiser Shorts Poster uses YouTube API Services. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google security settings page or from TikTok's app settings. Withdrawing access stops all uploads. To have the stored sign-in token deleted, revoke access there or contact us; it is removed from the owner's computer within 7 days. The app keeps no other YouTube data.
